## 4. Mechanical & Ergonomic Construction
- **Gimbal & Yoke:** Precision CNC-machined magnesium-aluminum unibody hinge with dampening friction washers for silent rotation.
- **Headband:** Cantilever spring steel core wrapped in breathable ribbed acoustic knit to eliminate crown pressure hotspots.
- **Ear Cushions:** Replaceable slow-rebound memory foam with acoustic mesh grills; magnetic snap-attachment system.
- **Physical Controls:**
  - Tactile continuous milled rotary crown (volume, track navigation).
  - Dedicated multi-function acoustic profile toggle button.
  - Physical hardware microphone mute slider.

---

## 5. Connectivity & Power
- **Wireless Standard:** Bluetooth 5.4 with LE Audio & LC3 PlusCodec support.
- **Wired Passive Bypass:** 3.5mm analog jack with direct transducer bypass (functions without battery power).
- **Digital Lossless Audio:** USB-C audio input supporting up to 24-bit / 192kHz PCM DAC decoding.
- **Battery System:** 1100 mAh Li-ion cell providing up to 45 hours continuous playback with fast-charge (15 mins for 6 hours).
