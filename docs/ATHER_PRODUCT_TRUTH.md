## 2. Verified Technical Specifications (Ather 450 Platform)

### Powertrain & Dynamics
- **Motor Type:** Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM) Mid-Drive
- **Peak Power Output:** 6.4 kW (Ather 450X) / 7.0 kW (Ather 450 Apex)
- **Continuous Power:** 3.3 kW
- **Peak Wheel Torque:** 26 Nm instantaneous torque
- **Transmission:** Zero-maintenance Gates carbon-fiber reinforced synchronous belt drive
- **Acceleration (0 – 40 km/h):** 3.3 seconds (Ather 450X) / 2.9 seconds (450 Apex in Warp+ mode)
- **Top Speed:** 90 km/h (electronically governed)
- **Gradeability:** 20 degrees (conquers steep urban flyovers and ramps with pillion)

### Energy Storage & Charging
- **Battery Chemistry:** High-energy-density Lithium-ion NMC cells
- **Pack Capacity:** 3.7 kWh nominal installed capacity
- **Ingress Protection:** IP67 certified (dust-tight and submersible up to 1 meter water immersion)
- **Thermal Architecture:** Die-cast aluminum casing with active structural potting and thermal dissipation
- **Certified TrueRange™:**
  - Eco Mode: ~150 km
  - Ride Mode: ~110 km
  - Sport Mode: ~90 km
  - Warp Mode: ~75 km
- **Ather Grid™ Fast Charging:** 0 to 80% at up to 1.5 km of range added per minute
- **Home Charging (Ather Dot):** 0 to 80% in approximately 4 hours 30 minutes (household 5A socket)

### Structural & Chassis Architecture
- **Chassis Structure:** Hybrid precision die-cast aluminum subframe paired with high-tensile steel trellis
- **Weight Distribution:** 47:53 front-to-rear distribution with ultra-low polar moment of inertia
- **Kerb Weight:** 111.6 kg
- **Suspension:** Telescopic hydraulic front fork with symmetrically mounted monoshock rear suspension
- **Braking System:** Hydraulic disc brakes (Front: 200mm ventilated rotor / Rear: 190mm rotor) with Combined Braking System (CBS)
- **Regenerative Braking:** Magic Twist™ reverse-throttle electronic energy recuperation

### Intelligence & Software (AtherStack™ 6.0)
- **Cockpit Display:** 7.0-inch capacitive multi-touch TFT display
- **Display Ingress:** IP65 water and dust resistant, chemically strengthened glass with optical bonding for direct sunlight visibility
- **Processing Unit:** Qualcomm Snapdragon quad-core processor with 2GB RAM and 16GB eMMC storage
- **Operating System:** Android-based AtherStack™ real-time vehicle operating system
- **Connectivity:** 4G LTE eSIM, Bluetooth 4.2 LE, GPS / GLONASS
- **Key Software Functions:**
  - Real-time onboard Google Maps with turn-by-turn navigation and charger routing
  - Dynamic Ride Modes (Eco, Ride, Sport, Warp)
  - AutoHold™ automatic brake-free incline holding
  - FallSafe™ real-time motor cut-off upon lean angle sensor trigger
  - Over-the-air (OTA) remote firmware deployment
