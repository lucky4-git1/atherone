# Aether One // Content Strategy & Editorial Guidelines

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone

| Trait | How It Sounds | What It Rejects |
|---|---|---|
| **Confident** | Direct statements about acoustic physics and materials | Exaggerated hype, exclamation marks, boastful claims |
| **Restrained** | Concise sentences with high information density | Verbose marketing paragraphs, filler copy |
| **Precise** | Exact millimeter dimensions, materials, frequency ranges | Vague promises like "experience next-gen immersion" |
| **Honest** | Explicitly labeled conceptual portfolio demonstration | Pretending to be an actual commercial manufacturer |

---

## 2. Banned Words & Replacements

| Banned AI Buzzword | Recommended Replacement |
|---|---|
| *Revolutionary* | Custom-engineered, purpose-built |
| *Unparalleled* | Calibrated, measured |
| *Next-generation* | Planar-magnetic architecture |
| *Immersive* | Natural acoustic soundstage |
| *Ultimate* | Flagship circumaural design |
| *Cutting-edge* | CNC-machined magnesium-aluminum |

---

## 3. Section Messaging Hierarchy

1. **Hero:** Instant product clarity. Flagship wireless audio headphones.
   - *Headline:* "Sound, engineered as an experience."
   - *Subheadline:* "Circumaural acoustic headphones with custom 45mm planar-magnetic drivers, machined magnesium chassis, and uncolored transparency."
2. **Introduction:** Acoustic philosophy and design rationale.
3. **Exploration:** Interactive 3D inspection with finish personalization.
4. **Engineering:** Exploded transducer anatomy and mechanical construction.
5. **Audition:** Sound profile laboratory with interactive Web Audio synthesis.
6. **Finishes:** Obsidian Black, Matte Silver, and Warm Sand editions.
7. **Comparison:** Standard Edition ($449) vs. Studio Reference Bundle ($549).
8. **Purchase Prototype:** Functional slide-over bag, item modifiers, demo checkout.
9. **Support & Care:** Physical maintenance guidelines, setup guide, conceptual warranty.
10. **Conclusion:** Unambiguous closing action to configure and explore.
